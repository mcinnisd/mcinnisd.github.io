SO-101 leader/follower arms In progress

I built two SO-101 arms and run them as a LeRobot-style leader/follower pair. They teleoperate. I am training a model in simulation, then I will collect episodes on a simple task and fine-tune a policy. The point of that order is to look at the sim-to-real gap.

As of October 2026. No results to report yet.

What the rig is

The SO-101 is an open-source arm from The Robot Studio, meant to be used with Hugging Face LeRobot. In the usual leader/follower setup, one arm is moved by hand and the other tracks it. That is the setup here, and teleoperation is working.
